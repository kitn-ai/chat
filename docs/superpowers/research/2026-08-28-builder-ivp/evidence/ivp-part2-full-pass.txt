=== Process hygiene ===
Every server was started in its own directory, one at a time (ports
4400/4401 are fixed, not parameterized). Each was killed after use;
`lsof -i:4400 -i:4401 -i:5173` and `pgrep -afl
"vite|mcp.js|construct-cli"` confirmed no orphans after every step and at
the end of the full run. One methodology note: a graceful `kill` (SIGTERM)
of the dev-builder process did NOT terminate it within 2+ seconds in one
observation — `kill -9` was needed, and the vite preview child (port 4401)
survived its parent's SIGKILL and needed a separate `kill -9` by its own
PID. This is noted as an observation for the implementer to consider
(whether SIGTERM should be handled to also tear down the spawned preview
child) but is not scored as a launch-blocking defect since a real user's
Ctrl-C sends SIGINT to the whole foreground process group, which was not
independently tested here.
